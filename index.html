Which areas do you serve?

We clean in Edmonton, Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Fort Saskatchewan and Ardrossan.

What types of cleaning do you offer?

Residential, commercial and office, real estate and steam cleaning, plus move-in and move-out, tenant turnover, post-construction and renovation clean-up.

What is steam cleaning, and is it chemical-free?

Our high-temperature steam cleaner lifts dirt and grease and removes 99.9% of bacteria without harsh chemicals. It works well on kitchens, bathrooms, upholstery, grout and high-touch surfaces.
